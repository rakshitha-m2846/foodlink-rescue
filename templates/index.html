How FoodLink Rescue Works

1

Restaurant Lists Food

Any restaurant or food business submits their surplus food via the simple form above.

2

NGO Browses Listings

Registered NGOs and volunteers browse the live listings page to find available donations nearby.

3

Food Gets Collected

The NGO travels to the pickup location, collects the food, and marks the listing as claimed.
